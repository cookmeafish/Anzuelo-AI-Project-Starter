# How to use this project

You talk. The AI does the rest.

1. **Open this folder** with your AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any other).
2. **First time only:** say **"set up this project"**. It asks you a few questions, then builds everything.
3. **After that, just ask** for what you want, in your own words.

That's it. You don't need to read anything else.

## Good to know
- **If it gets something wrong, tell it, however you put it.** It fixes the mistake, then stores the lesson where it
  stops the next one (a step in the skill that missed it, a rule, an automatic check) and tells you where, in a
  "Learned:" line. Lessons about the starter itself reach your other projects too.
- **If you want something done a certain way every time,** say "from now on, always ...". It turns that into a rule.
- **Short codes are optional.** Say "show me the menu" to see them.
- **Not sure where things stand?** Ask "where are we?"
- **Want it to check itself?** Say "run the sweeps".
- **It keeps itself lean.** It learns from its mistakes and asks before removing skills you no longer use.
