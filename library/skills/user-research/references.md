# User research: formats

## Study plan
```markdown
# Study: <name>
Decision it informs: <what will change depending on the answer>
Questions: 1. <..> 2. <..> 3. <..>
Method: <interviews / usability test / survey / card sort / diary study / A/B test>, because <reason>
People: <how many, which kinds, how found>. Missing: <who isn't covered>
Consent and storage: <how consent is asked, where notes live, who can see them>
Dates: <YYYY-MM-DD to YYYY-MM-DD>
```

## Interview guide
```markdown
# Interview guide: <study>
Opening (about 5 min): who we are, why we're talking, how notes are used, consent to record.
Today (about 10 min): "Walk me through the last time you <did the task>." Probe: what happened next, what was hard.
Deep part (about 20 min): <3 to 5 open questions on the topic, each with one or two follow-ups>
Reaction, optional (about 10 min): <sketch or prototype>. "What do you expect this does?"
Close (about 5 min): "What should I have asked?" Thanks, and what happens next.
```

## Synthesis report
```markdown
# Findings: <study> (<method>, <N> people, <YYYY-MM-DD to YYYY-MM-DD>)
Notes: <folder or files the findings come from>

## What to do
1. <recommendation>: based on themes <n>, <n>

## Themes
### <theme name> (<n> of <N>)
<what it is, in two or three sentences>
- "<exact quote>" (P<n>, <file>)
- Observed: <what people did>. Interpreted: <what we think it means>
- Means for the product: <..>

## Insights and options
| Insight | Option | Impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|

## Groups with different needs (only if the data shows them)
| Group | Who | Needs | Count |
|---|---|---|---|

## Limits and open questions
- Who took part, who didn't, how they were found, and the method's blind spots
- Still unknown: <..>. Next study: <..>
```
